Creamy Truffle Pasta

⏱ Prep time: 10 mins 🍳 Cook time: 15 mins 🍽 Servings: 2

There is nothing quite like the earthy, luxurious flavor of truffle paired with fresh pasta. This creamy truffle pasta is an indulgent, soul-satisfying dish that comes together in less than 30 minutes. It's the perfect recipe to impress a dinner guest or to treat yourself after a long week!

Ingredients

  • 250g fresh tagliatelle or fettuccine
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 200ml heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp truffle paste or truffle oil
  • 50g freshly grated parmesan cheese
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Instructions

Step 1: In a large pot of boiling salted water, cook the fresh pasta until al dente (usually 3-4 minutes). Reserve ½ cup of pasta water before draining.

Step 2: In a large skillet or pan, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté until fragrant, about 1 minute.

Step 3: Lower the heat and slowly pour in the heavy cream. Let it simmer gently for a couple of minutes until it starts to thicken slightly.

Step 4: Stir in the truffle paste (or oil) and the parmesan cheese. Stir continuously until the cheese has melted and the sauce is smooth.

Step 5: Toss the drained pasta into the sauce. Add a splash of the reserved pasta water if needed to loosen the sauce. Mix well until every strand is beautifully coated.

Step 6: Serve immediately, garnished with chopped fresh parsley, extra parmesan, and a crack of black pepper.
